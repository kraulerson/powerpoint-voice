## Smallest fix

Findings 2 and 6 share one fix — and 6 must land first, because the tester's own remedy ("make Continue keyboard-only") is currently **unimplementable**: there is no keyboard route to the gate.

1. **Wire the gate to the keyboard** (unblocks everything else): give `RecognizerController` a `setPaused(bool)`, call `window_->setPaused(...)` + `voiceGate_->setPaused(...)` from `AppShell`'s keyboard command sink for `Pause`/`ContinuePresentation`, and pass the real pause state into `noticeForRole` at `app_shell.cpp:416` instead of the literal `false`.
2. **Drop the four un-pause phrases** from `grammarPhrases()` (`vosk_recognizer.cpp:41-44`) and their arm of `matchCommand` (`command_matcher.cpp:95-100`). This breaks the kill chain at its only entry point, and removing `resume`/`continue` from the 40-word vocabulary also shrinks what OOV audio can land on.

Do **not** apply the `[unk]` suggestion as written — it turns voice off (2e).
